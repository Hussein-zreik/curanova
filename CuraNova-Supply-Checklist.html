How to use this sheet — 5 field rules

  • Category over brand. Stock each category, then one or two brands. Don't buy one of everything — it expires before it's used.
  • Core-bag items (●) are the ~80%-of-visits kit. Restock these to par after every shift; everything else weekly.
  • Order-backed items (debriders, antibiotics, sharps, biologics, anaesthetic) are stocked only against a physician order or standing protocol.
  • ABI before compression, always. No compression kit leaves the bag for a new leg ulcer until the ankle-brachial index clears it.
  • Let the data forecast. High-exudate wounds burn foams & superabsorbents; infected wounds burn antimicrobials. Use CuraNova's exudate/infection scores to set reorder volume, and localise brand names to what your market actually carries.
