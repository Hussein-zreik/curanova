Patient Log

Assessments save here automatically by MRN
Each patient is keyed by MRN. Saving an assessment whose MRN already exists appends the visit to that patient's record — it never creates a duplicate. Records are stored securely in this browser only.
Tap a patient to open their full record.
Evidence base. Diabetic foot — IWGDF 2023 Classification Guideline (SINBAD, WIfI); IWGDF/IDSA 2023 infection classification; ADA Standards of Care in Diabetes 2025 (comprehensive foot exam, 10-g monofilament). Surgical — CDC/NHSN Surgical Site Infection surveillance definitions; surgical wound classification (Clean → Dirty). Pressure — NPIAP/EPUAP/PPPIA 2019 International Guideline staging; Braden Scale for Predicting Pressure Sore Risk (Bergstrom & Braden). Wound-bed documentation follows the MEASURE / TIME frameworks.

Intended use: a structured aid for trained clinicians. Scores support triage and communication with the referring physician; they do not replace clinical judgement or physician diagnosis.
